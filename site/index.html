KYVON

A personal AI assistant that runs on a server you control. Chat, tasks, memory, and reminders, and it asks before it acts.

Early release. The iPhone app has been compiled and unit-tested by an automated build, but it has not been tried on a real phone yet. The web app version is the most tested way to use KYVON today.

1. You need a KYVON server first

KYVON is an app for a server that you run. The app does nothing until you point it at one. Pick whichever you like:

Quick local try

You need Python 3.12 or newer, and a Groq API key (create one at console.groq.com).

git clone https://github.com/temcwilliams/jarvis-assistant.git kyvon
cd kyvon
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
cp .env.example .env          # then put your GROQ_API_KEY in .env
.venv/bin/flask --app wsgi kyvon db-upgrade
.venv/bin/flask --app wsgi kyvon create-user     # choose your username and password
.venv/bin/python app.py                          # opens on http://localhost:8080

Windows is untested. If you try it, the commands are usually .venv\Scripts\python and .venv\Scripts\flask in place of .venv/bin/.... The always-on setup below expects Linux.

Always-on server (so your phone can reach it)

Run it on a small Linux machine or cloud VM, behind HTTPS. The repository's deployment guide covers the service file, backups, and a Cloudflare Tunnel for HTTPS. The app only talks to https:// addresses. Use a fixed address: a temporary "quick tunnel" address changes every time it restarts.

2. Get KYVON on your phone

Option A: the web app (free, easiest)

  1. On your iPhone, open your server's https:// address in Safari.
  2. Sign in with the username and password you created.
  3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

It then opens full screen like an app, with chat, tasks, memory, voice, and approvals.

Option B: the iPhone app (free sideload, from Windows)

Download KYVON-unsigned.ipa All releases

If the download says "not found", no release has been published yet. Check the releases page.

  1. Install iTunes (the version from apple.com, not the Microsoft Store one) and Sideloadly (sideloadly.io) or AltStore on your PC.
  2. Plug in your iPhone and tap Trust when asked.
  3. Drag the .ipa into Sideloadly and sign in with your Apple ID. The tool needs this to sign the app for your phone. Use an account you are comfortable with and read the tool's own privacy notes.
  4. On the phone: Settings, General, VPN and Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then Trust.
  5. Open KYVON, read and accept the data notice, enter your server's https:// address, and sign in.

Limits of the free route: the app stops working after 7 days and must be installed again, you can have 3 sideloaded apps at a time, and push notifications are not available.

Option C: the App Store

Not available. Publishing needs a paid Apple Developer account, and KYVON is not listed.

Your data

Your chats, tasks, and memories are stored on your server. The app has no ads, analytics, or tracking. To write replies your server sends your messages to an AI provider (Groq by default), and voice recordings go to Groq to be transcribed. The app asks for your agreement before it connects. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use. AI replies can be wrong, so check anything important.
