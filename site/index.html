Install

Early release. The iPhone app is built and unit-tested automatically but has not been tried on a real phone yet. Expect rough edges.
Windows or Mac · free

Install the iPhone app

  1. Download the file with the button above.
  2. On your computer install iTunes (from apple.com) and Sideloadly or AltStore.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, tap Trust, then drag the .ipa into Sideloadly and sign in with your Apple ID.
  4. On the phone open Settings, General, VPN and Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then Trust.
  5. Open KYVON, accept the data notice, and enter the server address you were given.
Free Apple IDs expire the app after 7 days: install it again to renew. Up to 3 sideloaded apps, and no push notifications. The tool asks for your Apple ID, so read its privacy notes first.
No computer needed · free

Use the web app

  1. Open your KYVON address in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Sign in with your username and password.
  3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
It then opens full screen like an app, with chat, tasks, memory, voice and approvals.

If the download button says "not found", the first release has not been published yet. Check the releases page.

Privacy

Your chats, tasks and memories live on your own server. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking. To write replies, the server sends your messages to an AI provider (Groq by default), and voice recordings go there to be transcribed. The app asks for your agreement before it connects.

Read the privacy policy and the terms of use. AI replies can be wrong, so check anything important.
