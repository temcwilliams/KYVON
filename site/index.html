Install

Early release. Nothing here has been tried on a real phone yet, so expect rough edges.
iPhone · free · no extra apps

Install KYVON on your Home Screen

Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, type your KYVON server address, and tap the button. Your address stays on your phone: this page builds the file itself and sends it nowhere.

  1. Tap Get the profile, then Allow when Safari asks.
  2. Open Settings. Tap Profile Downloaded near the top.
  3. Tap Install and confirm. iOS will say the profile is "Not Signed". That is expected: it only adds a Home Screen icon.
  4. Open the new KYVON icon and sign in.
If nothing downloads: open your server address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It gives the same result. This is the web app in an icon, not the native app, and the profile route is untested on a real phone.
Windows or Mac · free · needs a tool

Sideload the native app

Apple only lets signed apps install, and a free Apple ID can only sign through a tool like Sideloadly or AltStore. This is the only path to the native app without a paid developer account.

  1. Download the .ipa.
  2. On your computer install iTunes (from apple.com) and Sideloadly or AltStore.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, tap Trust, drag the .ipa into Sideloadly, and sign in with your Apple ID.
  4. On the phone open Settings, General, VPN and Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then Trust.
  5. Open KYVON, accept the data notice, and enter the server address you were given.
Free Apple IDs expire the app after 7 days: install it again to renew. Up to 3 sideloaded apps, and no push notifications. The tool asks for your Apple ID, so read its privacy notes first.

If the .ipa link says "not found", check the releases page.

Privacy

Your chats, tasks and memories live on your own server. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking. To write replies, the server sends your messages to an AI provider (Groq by default), and voice recordings go there to be transcribed. The app asks for your agreement before it connects.

Read the privacy policy and the terms of use. AI replies can be wrong, so check anything important.
