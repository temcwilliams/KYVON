# Legal drafts

**These are drafts written by an AI assistant, not legal advice, and nothing here guarantees that you
will not be sued or that Apple will approve the app.** Have a lawyer review them before you publish,
especially the limitation of liability, governing law and privacy sections, which depend on where you
live and who your users are.

Fill in every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder. The documents describe what the code actually does today
(checked against `kyvon/`), so if the data flows change (for example you start hosting servers for other
people, add analytics, or add an AI provider), the policies must change too.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) | Required by Apple, with a public URL |
| [TERMS_OF_USE.md](TERMS_OF_USE.md) | Terms of use; you may instead rely on Apple's standard EULA |
| [../appstore/APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md](../appstore/APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md) | Listing text, privacy "nutrition label" answers, age rating, export compliance, review notes, checklist |
