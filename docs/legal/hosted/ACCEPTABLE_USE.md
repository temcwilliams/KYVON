# Acceptable use

*Draft for legal review.*

You may not use KYVON to:

* break the law, or help someone else do so;
* create or share content that sexually exploits or endangers children, or that incites violence or hatred;
* harass, threaten, defraud, impersonate or deceive people, or generate spam or malware;
* attack, probe or overload KYVON or anyone else's systems, or try to get around limits, quotas or security;
* resell or share access, or run automated bulk use beyond your plan;
* process other people's personal data without a right to do so;
* use the service for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people without human review.

**What happens.** We may warn, limit, suspend or end accounts, and report serious illegal content to the
authorities. Suspension signs the account out everywhere and blocks sign-in; data is kept while we investigate and
you can ask us to review a decision at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

**Reporting.** Report abuse or a security problem to [ABUSE/SECURITY EMAIL]. We respond within [N] days.
