## What we collect

| Data | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, password | To create and secure your account | The password is stored only as a salted hash. We never see it again |
| Your content: messages, replies, tasks, memories, reminders, automations, notifications | To provide the assistant | Memories are saved only when you ask |
| Voice recordings | To turn speech into text | Sent for transcription; not kept as audio by KYVON |
| Approximate location (if you allow it) | Local weather and time zone | Used to answer; your time zone is saved, coordinates are not stored in the database |
| Usage counts (messages, tokens, voice clips, searches) | To apply plan limits and control cost | Tied to your account |
| Subscription status and Stripe customer ID | To know which plan you have | Card details go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us |
| Device sign-in tokens, device name | To keep you signed in; you can sign devices out | Only a hash of each token is stored |
| Technical logs: time, request path, result, an error code | Security, reliability, abuse prevention | Content of your messages is not written to access logs; error text is redacted of secrets |
| Terms acceptance time and version | To show you agreed | |

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies and we do not sell your data.
